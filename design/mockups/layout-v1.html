Why this is the honest answer

What we did look at

Workforce development, Department of Education, youth programs, small-business counseling, and community development. Those exist as technical assistance and local partnerships, not as $250K–$1M awards to a for-profit marketplace.

Adjacent, if you still want a public-sector path

  • SBA counseling and Utah GOED small-business programs (advice, not capital of this size).

  • If the product shifts toward workforce credentialing or measurable youth outcomes, re-run the map. The answer can change.

This is not a finding that you are ineligible for every public dollar. It is a finding that live federal grant listings are a poor use of the next 40–200 hours.
